about=Modern QGIS plugin inspired by MapAnalyst, built with Python, NumPy and SciPy (Qt5 and Qt6). Computes Helmert, affine and 2nd-order polynomial transformations between a historical map scan and a reference map with standard errors, cross-validation, Baarda outlier test, model comparison (AICc), spatial autocorrelation of residuals and Tissot-based local distortion analysis. Estimates the most likely map projection (Bonne, Cassini-Soldner, equidistant conic, Sanson-Flamsteed, trapezoidal, Mercator and more) and draws its graticule on the scan. Keeps a reference point library (GeoPackage) with regional point sets and per-map point quality, and compares maps: coverage matrix, time series of maps from different years (accuracy, position errors, residual similarity as a trace of copying, error vectors and trajectories) and multi-sheet publications, with PDF/HTML reports and charts. Rectifies maps with methods missing from the Georeferencer (global model plus a smoothed TPS correction field chosen by cross-validation, triangles, MLS), stitches multi-sheet publications into seamless mosaics (block adjustment with tie points and shared frame corners) and rectifies whole time series.
