about=Modern QGIS plugin inspired by MapAnalyst, built with Python, NumPy and SciPy (Qt5 and Qt6). Computes Helmert, affine and 2nd-order polynomial transformations between a historical map scan and a reference map with standard errors, cross-validation, Baarda outlier test, model comparison (AICc), spatial autocorrelation of residuals and Tissot-based local distortion analysis. Estimates the most likely map projection (Bonne, Cassini-Soldner, equidistant conic, Sanson-Flamsteed, trapezoidal, Mercator and more) and draws its graticule on the scan.
